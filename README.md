# Don’t Let the Chocolate Pile Up: Scaling DevRel Content with AI Agents

## Abstract
In DevRel, content demand can feel like Lucy on the chocolate factory line—as the pace speeds up, things quickly become impossible to manage alone. What if, instead of falling behind, you had a virtual team step in to balance the load?

This talk explores how to design a content “factory” powered by agents and skills, turning a one-person DevRel team into a coordinated production line. We’ll walk through a real-world system behind a weekly podcast and AMA series, where speaker coordination, content creation, and distribution are broken into stages and augmented by AI collaborators.

You’ll see how skills act as specialized stations on the line—automatically transforming livestreams into transcripts, summaries, and polished recaps—and how slide creation can be streamlined using tools like Marp, grounded by MCP servers. We’ll also look at how coding agents reduce maintenance overhead while sparking curiosity and deeper engagement from your audience.

Finally, we’ll zoom out to rethink DevRel in the age of AI: moving from handcrafted, instructor-led content to scalable, self-guided learning experiences built on repeatable systems. It’s time to stop chasing the conveyor belt—and start running your own agent-powered chocolate factory… without having to stuff the extra content in your hat.
