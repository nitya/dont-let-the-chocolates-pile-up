# Voice agents, upgraded: speech models in Microsoft Foundry

Speech is how a lot of people would rather talk to an agent. In S4-E9 of Model Mondays, host Gustavo Cordido and guests looked at what it takes to build a voice agent in Microsoft Foundry: the speech models underneath, and the experience of building, testing, monitoring and optimizing one end to end.

- **[Live demo: a voice agent for finance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBFQLt3StVI&t=854s)**: the longest segment (about 20 minutes). It walks through building, testing, monitoring and optimizing a voice agent in the Foundry playground, from start to finish.

- **[Speech models in Foundry: architectures and pipelines](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBFQLt3StVI&t=2688s)**: how speech-to-text, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech fit together in a voice pipeline.
- **[Customer use cases and Q&A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rBFQLt3StVI&t=3069s)**: how teams are putting this into practice, plus audience questions.

## Key takeaways

1. **A voice agent is a pipeline.** Speech-to-text, the agent, and text-to-speech (or a single speech-to-speech model) each shape how natural and responsive the experience feels.
2. **Build it like any other agent.** The demo treats voice as a full lifecycle (build, test, monitor, optimize), not a one-off prototype.
3. **Model choice matters for voice too.** The latest speech-to-text, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech models in Azure Speech in Microsoft Foundry, including new MAI models, are aimed at more natural, responsive conversations.
